BORGES LIBRARY - IMAGE PLACEMENT GUIDE

Place your images in the /images folder with these names:

narrator.jpg or narrator.png - solitary figure in a study surrounded by books
dreamer.jpg or dreamer.png - ancient circular ruins overgrown with jungle
yutsun.jpg or yutsun.png - figure at a train station, looking over shoulder
book-of-sand.jpg or book-of-sand.png - old book with pages fanning out endlessly
basement-shelf.jpg or basement-shelf.png - dusty shelf deep in a basement archive
dreamed-man.jpg or dreamed-man.png - ghostly human figure forming from smoke
god-of-fire.jpg or god-of-fire.png - flames forming a divine presence
dreamed-man-return.jpg or dreamed-man-return.png - figure dissolving in flames
revolver.jpg or revolver.png - vintage revolver on a train seat
manuscript.jpg or manuscript.png - ancient Chinese manuscript with branching diagrams
murder.jpg or murder.png - silhouette in a doorway or dramatic shadows

Then replace the [IMAGE: ...] placeholders in the HTML files with:
<img src="../images/filename.jpg" alt="description" style="max-width:100%;">

For character-select.html, use:
<img src="images/filename.jpg" alt="description" style="max-width:100%;">
